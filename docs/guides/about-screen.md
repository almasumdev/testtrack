# About screen: TestTrack's own rules

These sit on top of the shared guide (`mkit guide about`). Where the two disagree, this file wins for TestTrack.

## Community links

Community links on About use the real brand logos in brand colours on neutral tiles.

- This replaces the shared guide's "one outline icon set, one colour" rule for these links only. Every other mark on the page (envelope, globe on `eazyverse.com`, chevron, stars) stays as the shared guide says.
- The links sit in one bank, "Join the community", as a grid: four across, a short last row centred, never a lone tile pushed to the left.
- Each link is a 56dp tile with 16dp corners, filled with the neutral surface (`sheet`) inside a 1dp `cardLine` edge, the logo at 28dp in the middle, and its name under it (12.5 on 16, semibold, `text2`, up to two lines).
- Logos are copied from the brands' own marks (simple-icons paths) as vector drawables, never redrawn or recoloured: WhatsApp green `#25D366`, Telegram blue `#26A5E4` with its white plane, YouTube red `#FF0000` with its white play mark, Facebook blue `#0866FF` with its white f.
- GitHub's mark is one colour by its own rules: near-black in light, white in dark. It is the only logo tinted at runtime, with the page's text ink.
- The WhatsApp channel is WhatsApp's mark with a small green broadcast badge on its lower corner, ringed in the tile colour.
- The app's own website is the one mark that is ours: the outline globe in the accent ink.
- The button rule still holds: the label names the destination and nothing else. What is there goes in the line above the grid.
- Each link leaves the app and fails in silence, like every other control on the page. A link with no address yet is left out, and the bank is not drawn when it has none.
