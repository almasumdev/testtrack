# Deleting your TestTrack account

Send an email to **hello@eazyverse.com** from the Gmail address you use to sign in to TestTrack,
with the subject **Delete my TestTrack account**.

Nothing else is needed. The address you send from is the account, so there is nothing to prove.

Requests are actioned within 7 days and you get a reply when it is done.

## What is deleted

- Your profile: name, email address and picture
- The record of which cohorts you were in
- The push notification token for every phone you signed in on
- Every proof you posted, meaning the screenshot record and the seconds of use

## What is not deleted, and why

**The screenshots themselves are already yours.** They live in your own Google Drive, in a folder
TestTrack created under your own account. Deleting your TestTrack account does not touch them, and
we could not delete them if we wanted to. Delete that folder yourself if you want them gone. The
opposite is also true: delete the folder and the developers who were shown those images lose them,
because the file was never copied anywhere else.

**Your apps stay if a run is in progress.** An app of yours placed in a running cohort is
something twelve other people are opening every day. Withdrawing it mid-run costs them a day each
through no fault of theirs, so the app is taken out at the end of the run rather than the moment
the request arrives. If you want it out immediately, say so in the email and it will be done, and
the cohort will be told why their list got shorter.

## Deleting one app rather than the account

Open the app, tap the app on your home screen, and use **Withdraw**. That takes the app out of the
queue or out of its cohort without touching your account or your record as a tester.

## If you have not signed in yet

There is nothing to delete. TestTrack keeps no record of anybody who has not signed in.
