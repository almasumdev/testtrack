# TestTrack review, 30 October 2026: final

The app, the server and the admin panel, read through for wrong logic, missing logic and corner
cases, with a verdict on every item. **Status: final, all 29 items to be fixed.** Decisions made
while going through them are written under each item as **Decided (30 Oct)**; they win over the
suggested fix above them.

**Who checked what**
- Two independent reviewers read the code. Items S1 and A2 were also re-checked against the code
  by hand.
- Codex (GPT-6-Astra, high effort) started both reviews but stopped at its usage limit before
  writing findings; it can run again later as a second opinion.
- Web checks, all passed: the panel's 89 tests on desktop and phone; all 22 panel pages at desktop
  and phone size with no errors, blank values or sideways scrolling; the live panel refuses
  anyone not signed in; the live API is healthy and the public feed shows owners only as
  "A developer".

**The decisions in short**
1. Nothing new rewrites what was already judged (reports, skips, put back).
2. A report excuses only the night it is sent on (S2).
3. Put back: the out nights earn nobody a skip; the real misses before still do (S3); it checks
   room, the app and the owner first (S4).
4. Unusual tix activity is flagged for the team, not blocked (S6).
5. The team can extend a running crew's time; the app never assumes 14 nights (S9, A4).
6. Waiting pictures are kept per app per night, show red if they wait too long, and clear when
   they upload (A1).
7. Open all skips what will be refused and finishes the rest; what is left shows "1 left" (A3).
8. The admin panel offers ready-made reasons everywhere and never offers what the server would
   refuse (W7).

Severity: **High** costs tix or nights or blocks people; **Medium** is wrong but rarer; **Low** is
small or cosmetic.

## Server: tix and fairness

### S1. Removing a seat from a finished crew refunds it
Medium (staff actions only) · logic · `app/services/seat_out.py`, `app/services/crews.py` (`begun`), `app/repositories/removal.py`

When a crew ends, members who missed nights get nothing back. But the seats are not marked
finished, and for a finished crew the server's "has the run begun?" answers no. So if staff remove a
seat from a finished crew, the server treats it like leaving before the run and refunds 100 tix.

Three staff actions do it:
1. **Take out** on a seat in a finished crew. The panel's dialog says "No refund"; the server pays.
2. **Deleting a member**: each seat they still hold in finished crews is refunded into the deleted
   account. **Restoring** the account then leaves them with those tix.
3. **Deleting an app**: each of its seats in finished crews is refunded the same way.

Example: Rahim misses nights 5 and 9 in Crew 03, which ends; he rightly gets nothing. A week later
staff press Take out on his old seat, or delete and restore his account: he has 100 tix he never
earned.

Not affected: it pays at most once per seat, a clean-run refund is never paid twice, and no member
can do this themselves.

Fix: refuse Take out once the crew is finished; leave seats in finished crews alone when deleting a
member or an app; hide Take out on finished crews in the panel (W2).

- [x] Fix now
- [ ] Fix later
- [ ] Not a problem

### S2. A report excuses every night of the run, past ones too, with no limit
High · logic · `app/services/crews.py` (report), `app/services/visits.py`, `app/services/skips.py`

Reporting an app as "won't install" removes it from everything the reporter owed, on every night,
including nights already over.
- Someone who missed nights can report every app on night 14 and still get the 100 tix back.
- A member can report another app late to cancel that owner's quiet skips, so the owner loses the
  refund.

Fix: a report counts only from the night it was filed; cap reports per run, or let the team
confirm them.

**Decided (30 Oct):** a report excuses only the one night it is sent on, not the nights before it
and not the nights after. Nothing already judged changes: past nights, past misses and quiet skips
earned or used stay exactly as they were. A new report never rewrites old results.

- [x] Fix now
- [ ] Fix later
- [ ] Not a problem

### S3. Putting an app back gives others skips against its owner
Medium · logic · `app/services/skips.py`, `app/services/crew_appeals.py`

While the app was out, the server refused its owner's opens, so each night out counts as the owner
missing every other app. Each of those gives the other members a quiet skip of the owner's app.
Put back on night 8 after going out on night 6, and others can leave it unopened for about 4 of the
last 6 nights.

Fix: don't count the owner's out nights toward skips.

**Decided (30 Oct):** only the nights the app was actually out (from the night it was taken out to
the night it was put back) earn nobody a skip against its owner. The real misses before it was taken
out (the two missed nights that took it out) still earn the other members their skips, even though
putting it back clears the owner's own warning for those nights.

- [x] Fix now
- [ ] Fix later
- [ ] Not a problem

### S4. Put back doesn't check the app's place is still free
Medium · corner case · `app/services/crew_appeals.py`

An app that is out frees its place. If the team has put another app in, or the owner has put the
same app into a new crew, putting it back can make 16 apps in a crew, the same app in two crews,
or two seats for one owner. Nothing is locked, so it can also clash with the nightly sweep.

Fix: lock the crew, and refuse if it already has 15 apps, if the app is already in a crew, or if
the owner already holds another seat in it.

**Decided (30 Oct):** fix, and keep the app and panel saying the same thing:
- Panel: Put back explains why it was refused ("Crew 05 is full", "This app is in Crew 07 now"),
  and is not offered when it can't succeed.
- App: Ask to get it back is not offered once the app is in another crew; asking is refused with
  the same words; the owner's card never promises a put back the server would refuse.
- Review the app's and panel's appeal and put-back screens for any other place they disagree with
  the server, as part of the fix.

- [x] Fix now
- [ ] Fix later
- [ ] Not a problem

### S6. Starter tix trust the phone ID the app sends

new Google account. The one-review-per-phone and one-seat-per-phone rules rest on the same ID.

**Decided (30 Oct):** leave the phone ID as it is; the tix ledger already shows where every tix came

- many new accounts getting starter tix in a short time, or from the same network;
- an account that gets starter tix and spends them all at once on one other member's requests;
- any unusually large or fast run of tix movements for one account.
Nothing is blocked automatically; the team looks and decides.

## Android app

### A1. Night pictures are kept one per app, not one per night
High · logic · `data/VisitPictures.kt`, `viewmodel/NightPictures.kt`, `ui/CrewViewModel.kt`

A picture still waiting from night 5 is deleted when the same app is opened on night 6, so night 5
can never count. A late picture can also land on the wrong night, because the night is not sent.
Open all also skips an app while its old picture waits.

Fix: keep pictures per app per night, and send the night with each.

**Decided (30 Oct):** fix. The phone keeps every waiting picture (one per app per night) until it
is sent. If a picture waits too long, it shows red: on the member's row and as a waiting picture
in the owner's and the panel's night grids. As soon as the phone is back online it uploads them,
each to its own night, and the red goes away by itself.

- [x] Fix now
- [ ] Fix later
- [ ] Not a problem

### W7. Ready-made reasons everywhere the panel asks for one

## Checked and fine

- Every tix movement is locked, can't go below zero, and refunds are paid only once.
- Review approval, refusal, disputes and juror pay all balance; nobody is paid twice.
- Auto-approval: a closed test after a day; a live app after a day once kept installed, or two days.
- The update gate: forced and optional updates, offline replay.
- Opening a request is a preview until Install; My reviews only after the app is installed.
- The update row shows Update from the moment it's announced, with its deadline.
- Quiet skips show only as a small grey number.
- Every admin action checks its role and writes an audit row; the panel's buttons match the roles.
- The panel's data is never served from Cloudflare's cache.
