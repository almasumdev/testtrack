TestTrack: levels in the app bar and on people's rows

The final design. You earn exp by verified testing, reaching level n pays 3 × n tix, and from level 4 you can sit on a jury. Your own level lives in the app bar. Everyone else's level shows as a small chip next to their name.

  1. App bar. The robot with your level and progress on the left, your tix balance on the right.
  2. Level chip on rows. A quiet "Lv 7" chip after a person's name. Level 1 has no chip.
