Nightly guide

Open tonight’s apps

  • Use Open all, or open one app at a time from its crew.
  • Each app stays open for 20 seconds. It needs at least 10 seconds in front and a screenshot to count.
  • Finish before your local midnight. Pause the floating timer if you need longer.

Make each open count

  • Install from Google Play using the account in the testers group.
  • Allow usage access, connect your Google Drive, and approve Android’s screen sharing for the round.
  • If an update is announced, get it within 24 hours. After that, the old copy stops counting.

Finish the picture

  • TestTrack captures a picture while the app is in front. The open counts when time and picture are received.
  • Uploads retry automatically and keep the original night, even after midnight.
  • Missing picture? Open the app again or pick a picture. Picked proof is marked as added by hand.
  • Waiting more than 3 hours? Check your connection and use Try again.

Send private feedback

  • Once per app, say what worked and what could improve on Play, with screenshot proof.
  • Due by your night 7 with that app. After that, its opens stop counting until feedback is sent.
  • Crew feedback has no Tix reward.

Protect your run

  • A night is missed if 3 or more owed apps stay unopened, or you open none.
  • One missed night is a warning. 2 in a row take your app out.
  • Open every owed app on every owed night for a clean-run refund when the crew closes. One or two left can lose that refund even without a missed night.
  • Optional and excused apps are not owed. Once your own nights finish, nothing more is owed while other members finish.
