No review option on Google Play

  1. In Play, tap your picture. Make sure it is the account you installed the app with.
  2. Scroll down the app's Play page. If it says you are a tester, tap Leave and wait a few minutes.
  3. If it says Edit your review, you reviewed this app before. That review counts for nothing new.
  4. Update the Play Store app, then open the app's page again.
