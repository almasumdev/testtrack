Final: the rules card, closed and open

Built copy and numbers from CrewOpenRules.kt (2266fab). Open, the four headings are plain text and every bullet dot sits on the card's content edge, drawn here as a dashed blue line. A wrapped line hangs under its own text. Spacing: 16dp above each heading, 6dp from a heading to its first bullet, 6dp between bullets. No icons, no dividers.

Three options

Each card shows its key facts closed, and "All rules" opens the full, checked list in place. The open part is the same in all three. Figures come from CrewRules; the night count comes from the crew's run_days. Heights are measured under each frame.
