Crew, Open tab: Join a crew, and the seat sheet

The Open tab shows only what your account still needs before you can join, and nothing once you are ready. Whether an app is ready moves to the moment you take a seat: "Take a seat" opens a sheet that asks which app goes in, and walks that one app through what it still needs before the seat details and the confirm button.

Drawn in the accepted look from options.html, light and dark at 392dp, with a 360dp check for each part. App names, emails and icons are stand-ins. The line under each part's heading reports whether any row's text came out taller than its icon or got cut.

1. The Open tab

How it reads: the crew box keeps its header and seats. Under them, while anything is missing, comes "2 steps before you can join" with the account steps still to do as plain rows: the first one is open with its line under it, no box around it, and one "Next" bar sits under the list. The steps, in order, are Sign in, Join the testers group, Add an app in a closed test (only if you have no closed app at all), Connect Google Drive, and Have 100 tix. Done steps are not listed. When nothing is missing the box has just the terms and "Take a seat".

2. The seat sheet

How it reads: one PickerSheet, opened by "Take a seat". First, "Which app goes in?" lists your apps, ready ones first, each with one status line. Apps that can't go in are greyed with the reason and can't be picked. Picking an app shows that app's own steps in the same sheet, with the back arrow returning to the list: add the group to its closed track and verify, then two required boxes, then the seat details and the confirm button. Each unticked box says what goes wrong and how to fix it, and both lines go away once it is ticked. A ready app with both boxes already ticked goes straight to the seat details.

Notes

  • Why the boxes are per app: whether an app is in all countries and on all devices is set per app in Play Console, so the answer belongs to the app. It is asked here and on the app's own page, and kept once ticked. Both are required.
  • The boxes are the owner's word. Nothing checks them on Play; members trying to install still do, and two can't-install answers send the seat back, which is what the amber lines say.
  • Short of tix in the sheet: the Open tab only offers "Take a seat" with 100 tix or more, but tix can be spent elsewhere while the sheet is open, so the sheet still says it and holds the button.
  • The confirm button stays the filled pill (Primary), as on today's seat page: it spends 100 tix, and it is the only filled button in the flow.
