Crew, Open tab: the top as it is today

A record of what the top of the Open tab shows today: the notification button and the open crew card. Nothing here is a proposal. Copy, colours, sizes and spacing are read from the code at commit 9fa2c13 (13 Nov 2026).

Each state is drawn at 392dp, light on the left and dark on the right, in Barlow with the Ocean tokens from ui/theme/Tokens.kt. Each frame starts just under the app bar. The Crew tabs are drawn only to show where the top sits; the dashed strip at the bottom of each frame is where the guide cards begin (How-to video first), which this page leaves out.

Seat tiles show real app icons in the app. Here a coloured square with a white shape stands in for an icon.

1. The notification button

It sits above every Crew tab, Open included, under the tabs and above the card. It only exists while one of your crews is in its run. It is a plain Material text button in the primary colour: no icon, no card, no explanation line.

2. The open crew card

One CrewHero card: a chip, a headline, one line, the 15 seat tiles (8 on the first row, 7 on the second), and at most one full-width button. Seats confirmed on Play are solid, seats still being checked are faint, free seats are dashed, and the next free one carries a plus.

4. Things that do not change the top

These were checked because they sound like they should, but the code draws the top the same way for them. Where they show up is listed.
