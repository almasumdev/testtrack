Crew: the Take a seat page

The full page you reach from "Take a seat", as built today, then one recommended design in six states. Every frame shows the whole page from top to bottom with the button pinned under it. The pink dashed line marks where the first screen ends on that phone (412 x 915 and 360 x 800), so you can see what is in view without scrolling.

Colours, type and parts are the app's own: Ocean tokens from Tokens.kt, Barlow from res/font, Lucide icons, and composables that already exist (CrewOpenHeader, GroupAddress, GroupSteps, SeatBar, TwoChoice, DialogField, RowPill, Notice, Primary). App names and icons are stand-ins.

Today, as built

What reads as basic and uneven, from the code:

  • Three heading styles on one path. The seat page uses SeatHeading (15sp SemiBold). The Open tab and My apps use 16sp Bold (CrewHeading, SectionTitle). Next to each other the page looks like a draft.
  • The app at the top is small. FoundApp draws the name at 14sp, the size of body text, so the page has no clear top. My apps tiles use 52dp and 16sp.
  • The price is a paragraph. "A seat costs" with a long grey sentence, "100 tix" floating at the right, and one more sentence under it. Your balance is buried in the middle of a line, and short of tix only turns that line red.
  • No sense of where you are. Nothing says which part is done. A finished testers group still takes a heading and a row. The hint over the button only knows two things (group, boxes), so a disabled button with no reason is possible (empty name, half-filled sign-in, short of tix).
  • Spacing has no rhythm. 2, 4, 10, 12, 14, 18, 20 and 24dp gaps in one column.

Recommended One design, six states

Same order, same content, same logic. What changes:

  • The app on top as a My apps tile, without the box. 52dp icon with the closed-test flask on its corner, name 16/19 SemiBold, developer under it. The text column is 35dp, under the icon's 52.
  • One heading style, the app's CrewHeading look (16/19 Bold). Each heading can carry one short status at its far end: green "Play shows it" when the group is done, amber "Not shown yet" when Play said no, amber "40 tix short" on the details. You can see what is left without reading.
  • The testers group folds to one line when it is done (the owner's "steps only when something is missing"). When it is missing, the instruction is three numbered one-action steps (the shared GroupSteps, the same steps as Add an app and the How to add it sheet), with the address and Copy above them.
  • Seat details open with the crew row from the Open tab (CrewOpenHeader: crew name, seats left, likely start), so "where it goes" looks the same in both places.
  • The price becomes a row with the same 40dp tile as the crew row: "A seat is 100 tix", "You have 240 tix" under it. Short of tix turns that line amber and puts an "Open Circle" pill (the Open tab's soft RowPill) at the row's end. The seat rules follow as three short dot lines in the Open tab's rules style.
  • Before you submit stays last, with bigger checkboxes (22dp, 48dp rows) and the warning and the Play Console path under each unticked box, lined up with the label.
  • The hint over the button always says the first thing missing, in order: group, name, sign-in, tix, boxes. Tapping it scrolls to that part. When nothing is missing it says what the seat takes from your balance.
  • Spacing on 8: 32 between parts, 8 under a heading, 16 between blocks inside a part, 8 inside a block. No cards, no hairlines between parts, no box inside a box.

Loading and offline

Skeletons in the page's own shapes, no spinner. Offline with nothing loaded: one line and a Try again bar, no button.

Build spec

  1. App head (new SeatAppHead in CrewSeatSections.kt, replaces FoundApp and the "goes in with 14 others" line on this page only): a Row, centred. Box(size 52) with AppMark(pkg, name, icon, size = AppTileIcon, ownIcon = true) and SourceMark(live = false, Modifier.align(BottomEnd).offset(5.dp, 5.dp)), as AppCards.kt:96. 12dp, then a Column: name in AppTileName (16/19 SemiBold, text, 1 line, ellipsis), 2dp, developer (or package) in tileStyle(12.5f, 14f, Medium), text3, 1 line. 35dp of text beside 52.
  2. Headings: SeatHeading(text, status: SeatStatus? = null): Row, centred, min 19dp; text 16/19 Bold text; status at the far end: 14dp icon, 4dp, 12.5/14 SemiBold, one line, in ok (CircleCheck) or warn (CircleAlert). 8dp under it, none when the part is only its heading.
  3. Spacing: 8 above the head, 24 head to first heading, 32 between parts, 8 under a heading, 16 between blocks, 8 inside one. 20 under the last part, as now.
  4. Testers group, done: only SeatHeading("Testers group", SeatStatus.ok("Play shows it")). Open: heading with "Not shown yet" (warn) when checkFailed; intro 13/18 text2; GroupAddress(flat = true) (48dp row); GroupSteps("Roll out a release to that track."); 16dp; check line 12.5/17, text3, or warn with TriangleAlert 14; 8dp; SeatBar as now (Verify on Play, Asking Play, Check again).
  5. Seat details: heading with "N tix short" (warn) when short; CrewOpenHeader(crews.open) from CrewOpenTab.kt as is; 16; DialogField name; 16; SignInFields; 24; price row; 16; three rule lines; notices 16 apart.
