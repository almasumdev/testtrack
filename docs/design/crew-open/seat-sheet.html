Crew, Open tab: the seat sheet's app list

"Take a seat" opens the sheet on "Which app goes in?". Today it always opens at 82% of the screen, even for one app, and the rows look like settings rows with the apps that can't go in faded out. Below: the sheet as built, then three options where the sheet is only as tall as what it holds (up to 90% of the screen, then the list scrolls), each with 1, 3 and 8 apps. Light theme at 392dp; the recommended option also in dark, its other states and a 360dp check.

App names and icons are stand-ins. Each frame's caption gives the sheet's measured height. The line under each part says whether any row's text came out taller than its icon or got cut.

Today, as built

What the code does, and why it looks this way:

  • Always tall. CrewSeatSheet.kt:138 passes tall = true, so PickerSheet.kt:167 sets fillMaxHeight(0.82f) and the sheet opens fully at that height. tall is meant for a long list with search (Pick your app); this list has no search and most people have one to three apps.
  • Every view stretches. The list, steps and details each sit in Modifier.weight(1f) (lines 146, 182, 308), so each fills that 82% whatever it holds.
  • Loading fills it too. Line 310 draws ten 60dp skeleton bars.
  • The rows. Shared TileRow in a TileGroup with a 36dp icon. Apps that can't go in are faded to 50% as a whole (line 332), so the reason, the one thing worth reading there, is the hardest line to read. In light, the status line's ink (text3 #223D50) is almost the title's (#142F43), and only "Ready" has a mark. "Add a new app" is one more row in the same group. The app you went into and came back from is not marked.

Option A: grouped rows, sized to fit

The sheet wraps its list. Rows stay the shared TileRow, so they match the other pickers (Pick your app, Feedback on which app?). The app icon grows to 40dp, every status line gets a mark, and apps that can't go in move to their own group under "Can't go in now", at full strength with a greyed icon. "Add a new app" stays the last row of the first group.

Does well: smallest change, one flag on the sheet and a few row arguments. Same look as the app's other pickers.
Costs: still reads as a settings list. "Add a new app" weighs the same as your apps, and with 8 apps it lands in the middle of the sheet.

Option B: app tiles, with the add bar under the list Recommended

The sheet wraps its list. Each app is its own tile, drawn like a My apps tile at picker size: a 44dp icon, the name on one line, and one status line with a mark. Apps that can't go in sit under "Can't go in now" with a greyed icon and the reason in full. "Add a new app" leaves the list and becomes the quiet bar under it, the same bar as "Take a seat" on the Open tab, so it stays in view when the list scrolls.

Does well: your apps look the way they do on My apps, so the right one is found by its icon. The list and the one action are clearly apart, and the reasons are readable.
Costs: one new tile composable. Tiles are 66dp, so at 8 apps the sheet reaches the 90% cap and the list scrolls.

Option C: ready first, the rest folded

Same tiles as B. Apps that can't go in fold into one row, "3 apps can't go in now", which opens in place. "Add a new app" is the last tile. One app that can't go in is shown as it is, since folding one saves nothing.

Does well: the shortest sheet with many apps: 8 apps fit without scrolling.
Costs: the reasons hide behind a tap, which is where people look when an app is missing. One more state to build and test.

Option B: dark, other states, and 360dp

Build spec (Option B)

  1. PickerSheet.kt: add fitMax: Float? = null. When set (and tall is false), the column gets heightIn(max = window height x fitMax) instead of fillMaxHeight. Read the height from LocalWindowInfo.current.containerSize.height through LocalDensity. Other sheets pass nothing and do not change.
  2. CrewSeatSheet.kt: call PickerSheet(tall = false, skipHalf = true, fitMax = 0.9f, ...). skipHalf must stay true, or a long list opens half-way. Change the three Modifier.weight(1f) (lines 146, 182, 308) to Modifier.weight(1f, fill = false). Put animateContentSize() on the column that holds the when, so going from the list to the steps grows the sheet instead of jumping.
  3. CrewSeatList becomes: a scrolling column (weight(1f, fill = false)) with the pickable apps as SeatAppTiles 8dp apart, then, if any, the heading "Can't go in now" (13sp w600 text3, 16 above, 8 below) and the blocked tiles. Under the scrolling column, outside it: 12dp, then SeatBar("Add a new app", Lucide.Plus), then 16dp. While the list can scroll, a 1dp hair line sits on top of the bar's 12dp gap. Pass chosenId = pickedId in.
  4. New SeatAppTile (new file CrewSeatTile.kt): row, heightIn(min = 66.dp), corner 14 (TileRadius), background bg, 1dp cardLine border, padding 12 horizontal, 11 vertical. AppMark(size = 44.dp, ownIcon = true), 12 gap, column: name AppTileName (16/19 SemiBold, 1 line, ellipsis), 5dp, status row (12dp mark, 4 gap, tileStyle(12.5f, 14f, Medium), 1 line). Trailing: a 20dp box, 8 before it, holding Lucide.ChevronRight 18 text4, or Lucide.Check 18 accentInk when chosen, or empty when blocked. Chosen: leafAccentSoft over the background and the name in Bold, as TileRow(chosen) does. Blocked: no click, transparent background, icon through a 0-saturation ColorFilter at 45% alpha, name in text3. Semantics: one merged node, role Button, "Sunrise Notes, Ready to go in"; blocked ones read the reason and are not clickable.
  5. CrewSeatRules.kt: SeatNeed.NONE line becomes "Ready to go in". Add a SeatMark (READY, TODO, LOCK, WAIT) next to the lines: LIVE is LOCK, WAITING and APPEAL are WAIT. Marks: Lucide.CircleCheck ok, Lucide.CircleDashed accentInk, Lucide.Lock and Lucide.Hourglass in text4. Status ink: ready ok SemiBold, to-do text2, blocked text3.
  6. Loading: three skeleton tiles in the tile's frame (44dp square at corner 13, bars 14 and 11 high, 8 apart), with the add bar showing. Three, not SKELETON_ROWS: this sheet sizes itself, and ten would open it tall and then shrink it.
  7. No apps: subtitle "You haven't added an app yet.", one line "Add the app you want tested. It needs a closed test on Play.", then the add bar.
  8. Couldn't load (apps.offline with no apps): subtitle "Couldn't load your apps.", a line with TriangleAlert in warn, "Check your connection, then try again.", and SeatBar("Try again", Lucide.RefreshCw) calling apps.load(context). No add bar.
  9. Leave alone: the steps and details views (only their weight changes), TileRow, TileGroup, AppCard, the Circle picker in AppPicker.kt (it can take SeatAppTile later so both app pickers match).
