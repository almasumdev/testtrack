Start estimate: what the server sends and how the app says it

Field. On CrewOut (so on open in GET /crews): likely_full_at: datetime | None, the moment the 15th seat is likely to be confirmed, in UTC, and likely_basis: "pace" | "new" | None. The app works out the start from it, because the start is the first midnight after the crew is full in each reader's own zone (Crew.filled_at, services/days.py).

  1. Seats still needed. R = 15 - taken, live seats (checking or confirmed). Seats still checking count as taken, as the open list does.
  2. History rate. Take the last 10 crews with filled_at set and cancelled_at empty, filled in the last 120 days. For each, rate = 14 / hours(filled_at - created_at): a crew is created by its first seat, so its fill time covers the other 14. r_hist is the median. It needs at least 3 such crews.
  3. Recent rate. Over the last 7 days, across all crews: n = seats created, minus seats withdrawn or rejected in the same 7 days (by decided_at, or updated_at for withdrawn). r_recent = max(n, 0) / 168 seats an hour.
  4. Blend. w = n / (n + 8), so a busy week counts for more than a quiet one. r = w * r_recent + (1 - w) * r_hist. With fewer than 3 filled crews but n >= 5, use r_recent alone. With neither, send likely_basis = "new" and no time.
  5. Play check lag. lag is the median hours from created_at to decided_at for confirmed seats in those same crews, capped at 24. It is added once, because the last seat still has to be seen on Play.
  6. Result. hours = R / r + lag, at least 0.5. likely_full_at = now + hours, rounded up to the next whole hour. The rounding keeps the answer steady between reloads and works with the URL cache, since signed-out reads of this endpoint are cached.
  7. Hide it (both fields null): when the crew is not FILLING, when r is 0, or when hours is over 60 days. Older servers send neither field, and the app hides the line then too.
  8. Cost. Work it out once per crew and keep it for 10 minutes in memory. It is two small queries over the crews and seats tables, and the indexes they need are already there.

How the app says it. start is the first local midnight after the later of likely_full_at and now. days = round((start - now) / 24 h).
