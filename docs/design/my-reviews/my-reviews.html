1. What do I do next?2. How far along am I?3. What will I get, or what did I get?4. Does anything need me now?

Every phone shows the same 11 reviews: four in progress (steps 1 to 4, one held up because the developer can't find it), three waiting (one normal, one sent here at half pay, one disputed) and four in history (paid, refused, ran out, given up).
