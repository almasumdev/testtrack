TestTrack onboarding: three fresh directions

No robot. Same five messages, same words, same controls: Skip at the top, Next, Explore TestTrack on the last page, and Already here? Sign in under the button. Every phone is 360 by 800 with a status bar and gesture bar inside it, so what you see is what fits.

The colour is calmer: ink and paper, one blue (#32729B) for the button and the few things that matter, and coral and mint only as small touches taken from the icon. Dark is its own set: charcoal #0F1011, cards one step up at #17191B, soft #272B2F lines, a muted steel blue for text links.
