TestTrack onboarding: three calm illustration systems

1. Line and shade2. Shape and tone3. Line and dotThe robot
The screen stays exactly as it is in OnboardingScreen.kt: the brand row, the pager, the dots, the button and the sign-in link, with the agreed new words. Only the five pictures change. Each system below is shown twice, by day on the app's light ground and at night on its dark ground, and then the robot on its own.

What all three share
  1. Mostly ink and paper. One accent, robot blue, and only on the one thing each page is about.
  2. Dark is its own palette, not the light one turned inside out: fills one step above the night ground, soft grey-blue lines, a quieter blue.
  3. One stroke weight per system, no glows, no gradients, no dashed lines.
  4. The same robot in all three: a full figure with a neck, shoulders, hands and feet, and a pose that tells each page's story.

Why blue and not mint. The screen already spends its blue on the button, the dots and the label above the title. Blue in the picture keeps the whole page in one hue. Mint would be a third colour beside the brand row's coral and mint icon, and the aim is fewer colours. Blue is also the robot's own colour, so the accent always belongs to the character.
