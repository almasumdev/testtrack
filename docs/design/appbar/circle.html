TestTrack: the app bar on Circle

The top of the Circle screen with today's bar and three calmer ones. Each has a light row and a dark row. The first phone in a row is the whole screen; the others are the top of it, for the cases the bar has to handle: a new account at 0 tix, a long balance, an update waiting, an update ready, signed out, and still loading.

The bar has no notification bell today, so the only dot it ever shows is the one on the update button. The options keep that dot and draw it in robot blue instead of gold.

What today's bar does, from ui/TopHud.kt:

  • The level robot (LevelRobotBadge.kt): your level on a plaque, with exp to the next level drawn as a green line round it. It only does something when signed out, where it opens Sign in.
  • Two lines of text: "Level 7" and "120 exp to level 8". Signed out with a crew filling, they say "Crew 12 is filling" and "9 of 14 seats taken".
  • The update button when Play has one: download with a gold dot, a progress ring while it downloads, then a filled Restart pill that asks before restarting.
  • The tix balance: a raised navy pill with a gold ticket hanging off it. It rolls and hops when the balance changes. Tapping it does nothing.
  • Signed out: a Google Sign in pill in place of the balance.
