Full description (3940 of 4000)

Need 12 testers for 14 days? TestTrack is a free closed testing community where Android developers test each other's apps every day. Get 12 testers who really open your app, and honest feedback from people who build apps too.

On a new personal developer account, Google Play asks for a closed test with at least 12 testers, opted in for 14 days in a row, before you can apply for production access. (It used to be 20 testers.) Finding twelve people is hard. Keeping them opening your app on day nine is harder. TestTrack works because it is a closed testing community of people in your position: they need testers too.

HOW A CREW WORKS

A crew is 15 developers, each bringing one app that is in closed testing. While it fills, members join each other's tests on Google Play and share test logins. If an app won't install for others, two reports send it back to its owner to fix before anyone's 14 days begin.

When all 15 seats are taken, the crew starts at midnight in your own time zone. Each day you open the other 14 apps. Open all takes you through them one by one. Each app stays open for 20 seconds and TestTrack takes one picture of it at a random moment, so the round takes a few minutes. TestTrack reminds you in the evening if any are left.

Leave three or more apps unopened, or open none, and that day is missed. One missed day is a warning. Two in a row and your app leaves the crew. Open every app on all 14 days and your seat's tix come back in full. A crew has 15 seats for a rule that asks for 12, so there is room if a couple of people drop out.

WHY YOUR TESTERS STAY

• Everyone in a crew depends on everyone else, so they keep opening your app for the same reason you keep opening theirs.
• Every open is proven by Android's own usage records and a picture of your app, not by a button anyone can tap.
• Your crew page shows who opened your app each day, with the picture from each visit.
• Record keeps every finished run for when you apply for production access.

MORE PEOPLE TESTING YOUR APP

• Volunteers: no app of your own yet? Join a crew with free spots as a volunteer app tester. Open every app each night and earn 5 tix for every full night.
• Private testers: while your crew runs, add friends or colleagues by email. They test only your app, and only you see their results.

REAL FEEDBACK FROM OTHER DEVELOPERS

Daily opens help you meet Google's rule. They don't tell you whether your app is any good. In the Circle, other developers use your app properly and write to you about it: what worked, what confused them, what broke. It is beta testing from people who know the difference between a bug and a preference.

Testers use your app on two different days before they write, and each piece of feedback comes with a screenshot. You approve it, or raise a dispute that other members settle. You can also be an app tester for others, which is how you earn tix.

FREE, WITH NOTHING TO BUY

TestTrack is a testers community app with nothing to sell. Crew seats and feedback requests cost tix, TestTrack's own credit. New accounts start with tix, and you earn more by giving feedback or volunteering. No paid plans, no in-app purchases, no ads. TestTrack is open source.

WHO IT'S FOR

Indie developers, students and small teams publishing their first app on a new personal account. If you want a testers community app that keeps your test moving, or honest beta testing before launch, TestTrack is for you.

GOOD TO KNOW

• Usage access lets TestTrack time each app you test. It checks only those apps.
• Each night Android asks once to share your screen, so TestTrack can take one picture of each crew app. Pictures are stored on our servers.
• You can browse open crews and the Circle without an account. Joining needs a Google sign-in.

TestTrack is an independent app and is not affiliated with Google. Finishing a closed test helps you meet the requirement, but Google Play decides production access.
